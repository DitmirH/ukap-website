Studio basics

Sanity Studio is where all website content lives. The left-hand menu has two groups:

GroupWhat's inside
PagesThings that have their own web page: Custom Pages, Blog Posts, Events, Resources and the Donation Page.
ComponentsBuilding blocks that appear on pages: Hero Slides, What's On, Mission Section, Announcement Modal, Programmes, Contact Forms, Team Members, Authors, Sponsors and Sponsorship Tiers.
  • Open a list (e.g. Pages → Events) and click a document, or press the + / pencil icon to create a new one.
  • Edit the fields. Changes save automatically as a draft — the website does not change yet.
  • Press the green Publish button (bottom right). The website updates within about a minute.
Tip To take something off the site, use the menu next to Publish → Unpublish, or use the Hidden switch where a document has one (events, sponsors, programmes, tiers). Delete only when you're sure.
Photos Use landscape photos at least 1600px wide. After uploading, click the crop icon to set the hotspot — the part of the photo that must never be cropped (usually faces).

Development vs Production

The Studio has two workspaces. Switch between them with the dropdown at the top left of the Studio.

Development

Studio address ending /dev

Feeds the staging site (ukap-website-rose.vercel.app) and previews.

Use it to practise and try things out.

Production

Studio address ending /prod

Feeds the live site — ukapfoundation.org.

What you publish here, the public sees.

Check first The two workspaces don't share content. Publishing an event in Development will not put it on the live site — create it again in Production.

Quick task

Add an event

  • Go to Pages → Events and create a new event.
  • Fill in Event Title, press Generate next to Slug, then set the Event Date, Time (e.g. "6:30pm – 9:30pm"), Venue / Location and Full Address.
  • Add a Short Description (one or two sentences — it shows on the card) and a landscape Event Image.
  • Paste the Ticket Tailor link into Ticket / Registration Link and add the Ticket Price (or tick Free Event).
  • Pick an Event Category and a Card colour, write the full write-up in Full Description, then Publish.

The event appears on the homepage "What's on" and on the Events page by itself. Once the date has passed it moves to the Archive automatically.

Quick task

Post a news article

  • Go to Pages → Blog Posts and create a new post.
  • Add the Title, generate the Slug, choose an Author and upload a Main Image.
  • Set Published Date to today, write a short Excerpt and the article in Body.
  • Publish. It becomes the big "Latest" story on the homepage and appears on the News page.
Quick task

Change the homepage hero

  • Go to Components → Hero Slides and create or open a slide.
  • Add a Title, a short Subtitle / Description, a wide Background Image and up to two buttons.
  • Pick a Card colour and a Title & button colour, set Display Order, make sure Active is on, then Publish.

See Hero slides for the details.

Quick task

Add a resource

  • Go to Pages → Resources and create a new resource.
  • Details tab: add the Title (e.g. "Training materials — Finance 2026"), press Generate for the URL, pick a Category, write a short Summary, and optionally add a Cover image and Card colour.
  • Page content tab: build the page with text, tips lists, callouts, images, videos and links.
  • Downloads & links tab: upload files (PDFs, slides, templates, images…) and add useful websites.
  • Publish. It appears on /resources and gets its own page at /resources/your-url.

Page content

BlockUse it for
TextParagraphs, headings, bullet or numbered lists, bold, highlight and links.
Tips listNumbered tips in a bold black box — e.g. "10 CV tips".
Callout boxA highlighted note: Tip (gold), Info (blue) or Important (coral).
ImageA photo or diagram with caption.
VideoPaste a YouTube or Vimeo link — it plays on the page.

Downloads & links

Downloads — upload any file (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, images, audio, video, zip) with a title and short description. They're listed in a Downloads panel beside the article, with the file type and size. Useful links — websites, courses or tools, listed underneath.

Tip For a programme with several sessions (e.g. Finance 2026), make one resource and add each session's slides to Downloads, rather than a separate resource per file.
Note Uploaded files are public to anyone with the link — don't upload anything confidential or personal.

Custom pages

Studio: Pages → Custom Pages

Build any extra page (e.g. a programme page) at /page/your-slug. Add blocks in Page Content with the + button:

BlockUse it for
TextParagraphs, headings, quotes, bold/italic, links and the Highlight style.
ImageA photo in Small, Medium, Large or Full Width.
Contact FormEmbed one of the forms from Components → Contact Forms.
Call to ActionA button (primary, outline or text link) to any page or website.
Divider / SpacerA line or a gap between sections.
Two Column LayoutText and images side by side (50/50, 60/40, 70/30…).
Info BoxA highlighted note: info, success, warning or note.
Embed CodeTicket Tailor widgets, YouTube videos, donation forms.
Events ListA list of events, filtered by category, with or without past events.

Also set the Hero Image and Hero Style, tick Show in Navigation to add it to the menu (with Navigation Order), and fill in SEO Settings for search engines.

Donation page

Studio: Pages → Donation Page

One document controls the whole /donate page: hero title and image, introduction, "Why donate" items, impact statistics (e.g. "£8,000" / "Raised at our gala"), achievements, the donate button text and link, and any additional content. Sponsorship packages on this page come from Sponsorship Tiers.

Hero slides

Studio: Components → Hero Slides

The big photo at the top of the homepage, with a colour box overlapping it. The fixed "Opening doors to education" slide always shows first; your slides follow in Display Order.

FieldWhat it does
TitleThe big heading in the box. Keep it short — four to six words.
Subtitle / DescriptionOne or two sentences under the title.
Card colourThe colour of the box. Default: black.
Title & button colourOne colour for the title and both buttons, so they always match. Default: gold. The subtitle switches to black or white automatically.
Background imageThe wide photo behind the box. Use landscape, 2000px wide, and set the hotspot.
Primary / Secondary buttonText and link for each button, e.g. "/donate" or a full web address.
DurationSeconds before moving to the next slide (default 6).
Display order / ActiveLower numbers show first; switch Active off to hide a slide.
Good pairs Black + gold (signature) · Coral + black · Gold + black · White + blue.
Ignored The older fields Background Type, Background Colour, Custom Hex, Overlay Darkness and Text Alignment are no longer used by the design.

What's On (events cards)

Studio: Components → What's On (Events cards)

Controls the "What's on" section on the homepage. You only need one of these documents.

FieldWhat it does
Section titleHeading above the cards (default "What's on").
Which events to showAutomatic — the next upcoming events, soonest first. Hand-picked — you choose exactly which events and in what order.
How many eventsAutomatic mode only. Default 3.
Event cardsHand-picked mode only. Each card links to an event you pick; date, venue, photo and ticket link come from that event. You can override the card's colour, title, description or image.
"View all" link textText of the link to the Events page (default "All events").

Clicking a card or More info → opens the event page; Book now opens the ticket link. With no upcoming events, a friendly "New events are on the way" message shows instead.

Tip Leave it on Automatic day to day — new events appear and past ones drop off without you touching it.
Components

Mission section

Studio: Components → Mission Section

The black "Who we are" band on the homepage. Set the Eyebrow label, Heading, Mission text and button. Highlighted word puts one word of the heading in the coral highlight block — type it exactly as it appears in the heading. Make sure Active is on. (The four statistics beside it are fixed in the website code.)

Components

Announcement popup

Studio: Components → Announcement Modal

A popup on the homepage, e.g. for a gala. Set the Title, Message, optional Image, button text and link, and the small dismiss text. Choose How often visitors see it (every visit, once per session, once per day, or only once) and a Delay in seconds. Switch Active off when the campaign ends.

Components

Programmes

Studio: Components → Programmes

The "What we do" cards on the homepage: Title, Description, Image, optional Link and Display order. Use Hidden to remove one. (The old "Brand colour" field is not used by the current design.)

Components

Contact forms

Studio: Components → Contact Forms

Build forms (contact, mentor applications, volunteering) without code. Give the form a Name and Form ID, then add Form Fields: text, email, dropdowns, checkboxes, file uploads and more. Each field has a label, placeholder, required switch, half-width option and help text. Add text above or below with Header / Footer content, then place the form on a page with the Contact Form block. An email field is always included so you can reply.

Careful Don't change a field's Field Name (ID) after the form is live — it's how answers are labelled in the emails you receive.
Components

Team members & authors

Studio: Components → Team Members / Authors

Team Members fill the Team page: name, role, category (Lead, Volunteer, Advisor), photo, short bio, LinkedIn and display order. Use square, well-lit head-and-shoulders photos.

Authors are the names shown on news articles — name, photo and role.

Components

Sponsors & tiers

Studio: Components → Sponsors / Sponsorship Tiers

Sponsors appear in the scrolling partner logos above the footer and on the Partners page. Each needs a Company Name, Company Logo (required — a transparent PNG or SVG works best), Sponsor Tier, optional tagline, description, website and social links, plus Featured, Display order and Hidden.

Sponsorship Tiers are the packages on the Donation page: tier name, price, short description, What's included list, an enquiry link, and Highlight this tier to make one stand out.

Troubleshooting

ProblemCheck
My change isn't on the siteDid you press Publish? Are you in the right workspace (Production for the live site)? Wait a minute, then refresh.
My event isn't in "What's on"Is the date in the future? Is Hidden off? In Automatic mode only the next few (default 3) show — the rest are on the Events page.
No Book now buttonAdd a Ticket link; check the event isn't past, sold out or cancelled.
Article in the wrong placeOrder comes from Published Date — adjust the date.
Photo cropped badlyOpen the image, click the crop icon and move the hotspot over the important part.
Can't see a new fieldThe Studio may need redeploying by a developer (npx sanity deploy).
